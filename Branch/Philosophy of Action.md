Philosophy of Action studies what distinguishes actions from mere happenings, and examines intention, agency, reasons for action and [[Free Will|free will]].

**Key questions**
- What makes a bodily movement an action?
- Are reasons causes of actions?
- Are we free and responsible for what we do?
