Philosophy of Science studies the foundations, methods and implications of science: what scientific [[Knowledge|knowledge]] is, how theories are tested, and what they tell us about reality.

**Key questions**
- What distinguishes science from non-science?
- How are theories confirmed or refuted?
- Do scientific theories describe reality?
