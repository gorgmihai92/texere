Philosophy of Technology examines the nature of technology and its effects on human life, society and [[Knowledge|knowledge]], including computing, information and [[Artificial Intelligence (AI)|artificial intelligence]].

**Key questions**
- Is technology neutral?
- How does technology shape human agency and society?
- What responsibilities come with designing technologies?
