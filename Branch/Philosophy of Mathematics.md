Philosophy of Mathematics asks what mathematical objects are, how mathematical [[Knowledge|knowledge]] is possible, and why mathematics applies so well to the world.

**Key questions**
- Do numbers exist?
- How do we know mathematical truths?
- Can mathematics be reduced to [[Logic|logic]]?
