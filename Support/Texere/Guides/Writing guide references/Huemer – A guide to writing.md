# A guide to writing

## Key points

- Thesis plus arguments that are non-trivial, original, plausible and non-circular, plus objections answered.
- Be forthcoming (thesis at the start, a topic sentence for each paragraph), organized, brief, specific, plain; give examples for every major thesis.
- Misused words: infer (not imply), refute (not deny), true (not believed), know (not believe), beg the question (not raise it).
- Cut needless qualifiers (“I think”, “it seems”) without swapping them for overstatement (“obviously”, “conclusively refuted”); no polemical language.
