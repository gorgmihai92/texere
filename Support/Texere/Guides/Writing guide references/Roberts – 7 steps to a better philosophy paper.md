# 7 steps to a better philosophy paper

## Key points

- State the question precisely; a thesis of one or two sentences; premises that imply the conclusion.
- Outline: introduction (issue, question, thesis, preview), body (background, argument), conclusion (thesis, loose ends); write the body first.
- Write for a dull reader; do not overreach; signpost constantly (“My argument shall have the following form…”, “One response to this objection is…”).
- Plain, concise language; support every non-obvious claim instead of “I believe that X”; no sweeping claims about history or humanity.
