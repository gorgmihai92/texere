# Writing philosophy essays

## Key points

- Thesis explicit in the opening paragraph; background at most a third of the essay, your arguments and replies at least two thirds.
- Structures for evaluating or defending an argument, and for defending or refuting a thesis: state, argue, strongest objections, detailed reply.
- One argument per paragraph; expound before you evaluate; objections and replies in separate paragraphs.
- Obscure writing is not depth: test whether a reader new to the topic can state your thesis and its support.
