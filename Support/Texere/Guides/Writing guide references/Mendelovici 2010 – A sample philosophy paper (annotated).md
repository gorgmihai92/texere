# A sample philosophy paper (annotated)

## Key points

- A plain title; an introduction that says what the paper is about, gives a roadmap and states the thesis.
- Only the background the argument needs; a quotation followed by your own explanation.
- The opposing argument presented clearly and as plausibly as possible, with its premises and conclusion explicit; then exactly where you disagree.
- Your argument, with controlled language and examples; the conclusion restated; the damage to the opposing position assessed; an objection answered; a short conclusion.
- Signposting along the way so the reader knows what is going on.
