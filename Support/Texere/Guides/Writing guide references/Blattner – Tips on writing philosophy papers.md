# Tips on writing philosophy papers

## Key points

- Clarity first; one central thesis stated at the start, not held back for suspense.
- The philosophical lawyer: argue as if before a rational jury whose members can answer every point; show the objection–reply cycle in the text.
- Fewer, deeper objections; each objection and each reply at least a full paragraph (no hit-and-run).
- Avoid autobiography (“first I thought…”), over-quoting, sweeping intellectual history (“perennial”, “long questioned”), feelings in place of reasons.
