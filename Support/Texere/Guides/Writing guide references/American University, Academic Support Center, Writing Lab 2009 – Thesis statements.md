# Thesis statements

## Key points

- General academic writing (not philosophy-specific). A thesis must be a full sentence, not a fragment or a question.
- No “I think” or “in my opinion” (they weaken the statement); no unrelated elements; no vague, muddled or figurative language.
- Poor: “Religion is the phoenix bird of civilization.” Better: a literal claim with its reason.
- Note: philosophy guides allow the first person to say what you do (“I will argue that…”); what they reject is opinion in place of argument.
