# How to write philosophy

## Key points

- Three principles: a specific thesis; reasons for every claim; objections and rival answers considered fairly.
- Introduction (a few sentences): the problem, the thesis, the plan (“First, I will…”). Body: the problem explained, the arguments, objections and replies. Conclusion: thesis restated, arguments summed up.
- An orderly discussion supported by evidence, not a summary with occasional comment.
- Plan in ten steps; write the body first; let the draft rest; expect at least two drafts.
