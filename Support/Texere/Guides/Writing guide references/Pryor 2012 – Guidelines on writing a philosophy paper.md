# Guidelines on writing a philosophy paper

## Key points

- A reasoned defence of a modest claim; outline first (most of the work).
- Make the structure obvious; use connectives only where they are true; keep your views distinct from those you report.
- Concise but fully explained, for a reader who is lazy, stupid and mean; simple prose; examples; definitions; consistent terms.
- Present others accurately and charitably; paraphrase rather than quote; anticipate the strongest objections.
- No warm-up openings, no dictionary definitions; “I” is fine.
