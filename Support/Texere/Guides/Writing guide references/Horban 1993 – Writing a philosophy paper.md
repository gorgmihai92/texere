# Writing a philosophy paper

## Key points

- A reasoned defence of a thesis you can state in one short sentence, written for an intelligent reader who disagrees.
- Develop one or two strong arguments in depth; the “fortress approach” (every possible argument) hides the good ones.
- Bad papers: long introductions about importance or history, long quotations, fence-sitting, flippant tone, begging the question.
- Use connectives (therefore, hence, since) only where the logical relation holds; choose exact words; edit heavily.
