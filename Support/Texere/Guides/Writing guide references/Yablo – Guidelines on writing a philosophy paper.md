# Guidelines on writing a philosophy paper

## Key points

- Adapted, nearly word for word, from Jim Pryor's guidelines of the same title (see [[Pryor 2012 – Guidelines on writing a philosophy paper]]).
- How papers are graded: a clear aim and thesis, visible arguments, exposition kept separate from your contribution, simple prose, good examples, others presented accurately and charitably.
- Philosophical writing differs: some conventions from other courses do not apply; precision closer to science than to the humanities; clarity over elegance.
