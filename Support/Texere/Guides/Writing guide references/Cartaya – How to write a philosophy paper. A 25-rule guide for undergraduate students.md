# How to write a philosophy paper: A 25-rule guide for undergraduate students

## Key points

- Four standards: writing quality, argument reconstruction, critical engagement, charity.
- Thesis explicit and contestable, followed by a roadmap; signposts (“I will argue that…”, “One objection is…”, “By contrast…”, “It follows that…”).
- Every sentence serves the thesis; consistent terminology (no synonyms for variety); conclusion looks back (what is still open) and forward.
- Reconstruction is not summary: list premises and conclusion, and the reasons behind each premise.
- A modest, original point; steelman the opponent; no loaded words (“clearly absurd”, “obviously ridiculous”).
