Philosophy of Religion examines religious concepts and claims, such as the existence and nature of [[God]], [[Faith|faith]], religious experience and the [[Problem of Evil|problem of evil]].

**Key questions**
- Can the existence of God be proved?
- Is faith rational?
- Why is there evil if God exists?
