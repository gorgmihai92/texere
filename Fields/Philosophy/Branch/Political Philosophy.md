Political Philosophy studies the [[Justification|justification]] of political [[Authority|authority]], the nature of [[Justice|justice]], [[Rights|rights]] and [[Freedom|freedom]], and how society ought to be organised.

**Key questions**
- What makes political power legitimate?
- What does justice require?
- What are the limits of individual freedom?
