Not a branch of philosophy. This note groups introductory and general works that cover philosophy as a whole rather than a single branch (PhilPapers: *Philosophy, Introductions and Anthologies*).

Use it in the **branch** property of introductory books, such as [[Craig 2002 – Philosophy. A very short introduction]] and [[Blackburn 1999 (2009) – Gândește. O introducere convingătoare în filosofie|Blackburn 1999 – Think]].
