Philosophy is the systematic, critical study of fundamental questions about existence, [[Knowledge|knowledge]], value, reason, mind and language.

This note maps philosophy as a field. Its properties list every branch, subdomain, historical period, movement, theory, concept and author in this vault. Each of them has its own note, with a short definition, that links back here. Open **Graph View**, or the **Backlinks** panel of any note, to explore the connections.

## How the map is organised

| Property | What it contains | Folder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| branch | the main areas of philosophy | Branch |
| subdomain | specialised fields within a branch | Subdomains |
| historical-period | the main periods in the history of philosophy | Historical Periods |
| movements | schools and traditions with a history, such as [[Stoicism]] | Movements |
| theories | positions that can be defended or rejected, such as [[Compatibilism]] | Theories |
| concepts | key ideas and problems, such as [[Justice]] or [[Consciousness]] | Concepts |
| author-links | philosophers | Author |

Some "-isms" are both a historical movement and a position (for example, [[Empiricism]]). In this vault each one appears only once, in the category where it is most often discussed.
