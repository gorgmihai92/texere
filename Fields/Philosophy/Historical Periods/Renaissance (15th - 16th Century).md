The revival of classical learning and the rise of [[Humanism|humanism]], bridging medieval and modern thought.

## Sub-periods
- **Italian Renaissance** (15th century): Florentine humanism and the revival of Plato (Ficino, Pico della Mirandola).
- **Late Renaissance** (16th century): political realism (Machiavelli), Christian humanism (Erasmus, More), [[Skepticism|scepticism]] (Montaigne) and the new [[Cosmology|cosmology]] (Bruno).

## Main themes
- [[Dignity|Human dignity]] and [[Freedom|freedom]]
- The return to classical sources
- Politics without moral illusions
- Scepticism and the limits of [[Knowledge|knowledge]]
- A new picture of the cosmos
